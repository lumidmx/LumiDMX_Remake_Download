Testing

For: FullPlayer

Without music

You can try a list without any sound: right-click a square.

  • A short right click = one touch: the list takes one step.
  • Right click held down = the sound stays in the square: the chase plays on by itself; when it has finished its own round, the list goes on to its next chase.
  • Release = the sound leaves the square: without Fade the chase's channels go dark, with Fade the light stays.

The right button held on the square of "Rainbow Games".

1The square is filled while it is touched.
2Next Step of its list lights up.
3The chase that plays now, with its step.
4The status line: "Rainbow Games": started "Rainbow Games - Curcubeu" (step 1).

With music

  1. Choose the input (for music played on this computer: its loopback - the speakers) and press Start.
  2. Set Master Volume or Input Level so that the bars move in the lower three quarters of the graph.
  3. Watch the squares: the ones that light up too often go higher or get smaller; the ones that never light go lower or get bigger, or move to where the music is strong.
  4. Look at the result on the lights - or, without lights, in the 3D (Launch 3D) or the DMX Board.

Music from the computer (loopback), Beat and Dir. Trigger on, the 3D beside it.

1Rec ON - the program listens.
2The status line - every step of every list.
3The current step of each list.
4The 3D shows what goes out.
5Launch 3D.

When nothing happens

  • Rec OFF - press Start (or Rec).
  • No bars - wrong input, or the volume at 0. For music of the computer choose its loopback, not a microphone.
  • The square lights but nothing steps - the band has no list (List: empty), or the list is paused (Pause), or its chases no longer exist (the status line says so).
  • The light goes dark between the beats - tick Fade on the list.
