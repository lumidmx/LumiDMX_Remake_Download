The sound

For: FullPlayer

Choosing the input

The Input list has two kinds of sources:

  • Inputs - a microphone, a line input, a sound card input: the sound of the room or of a mixer.
  • Loopback (shown with the name of a speaker or headphone output) - the music that plays on this computer, taken directly, without a microphone. The best choice when the music comes from the same computer.

Refresh reads the list again (after plugging in a sound card); the status line says how many inputs and loopback devices were found. Press Start to listen, Stop to stop. Rec ON in the bar at the top does the same from any tab: green = listening.

The levels

  • Master Volume - the volume of the computer's output. With a loopback input, the bars follow this volume: if they are too low or too high, change it here.
  • Input Level - the recording level of a microphone or line input (it does not act on a loopback: use Master Volume).
  • Level - how loud the sound is now; Raw is the same as a number.
  • Auto level: ON / Auto level: OFF (the small button next to Input Level) - ON (the default): every bar scales itself to what it heard in the last seconds, so the squares catch the music the same way whether it is loud or quiet; in silence the bars slowly climb to the noise of the microphone. OFF: a fixed scale - the bars show how loud the sound really is, silence stays at the bottom and only sound makes them climb; set the height once with Input Level (or Master Volume). Kept with the project.
  • Quiet: Slow Move ON / OFF (next to Auto level) - see below.

Set the level so that in the loud parts of the music the bars reach about three quarters of the graph and do not stay at the top: the squares then catch the beats and not everything.

Quiet: Slow Move

For the parts of a song without the beat - only the voice, a break, someone speaking - the lights can calm down by themselves, as when you stop Rec and press Slow Move on the Live Panel.

  • Quiet: Slow Move OFF (grey, the default) - nothing changes.
  • Quiet: Slow Move ON (blue) - when there is no kick drum for about 3 seconds, Sound2Light holds (its lists stop, what they started stops) and the Slow Move button of the Live Panel starts, with its own movement and colours. The graph shows QUIET PART -> Slow Move. When the kick comes back (two beats), Slow Move stops and Sound2Light goes on.
  • The kick is found in the bass (35 - 80 Hz): a voice, a held bass note or a pad do not count as the beat. Silence counts as a quiet part too.
  • Slow Move running already (pressed by you) is left on when the beat comes back.
  • Rec OFF: nothing happens from here - what is on stays on.
  • The arrow next to the button chooses another button of the Live Panel (by its first letter); the project's Slow Move is found by its name (any capitals). Kept with the project.

The EQ

The 12 sliders, from 30 Hz to 22.05 kHz, make a range of sounds stronger (up) or weaker (down) before the bars, the squares and the BPM use it. For example: the kick drum too weak - raise 50 and 100; the voice touching the squares too often - lower 1k and 2k.

Save as... keeps the 12 sliders under a name, the Preset list brings them back, Delete removes the chosen preset, Reset puts all the sliders back to the middle.

The BPM

  • Manual - you write the tempo (or use the arrows).
  • Tap - click it in time with the music; the mean of the last four clicks is the tempo (a pause of more than 2 seconds starts again).
  • Auto - found from the bass of the music; with Beat Trigger ticked it follows the kicks the Beat Trigger finds (from Beat Trigger).

The BPM is shown for you. The lists do not step on this number: they step when a bar reaches into their square (or on every kick, with the Beat Trigger).
