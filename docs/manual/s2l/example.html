Example: a new list from start to end

For: FullPlayer

A list that changes the colour rainbow of the beams with the bass, made on Andrei_edit.

  1. Open the Sound2Light tab, then the Library.
  2. In the Library, under Light Sequences, right-click Curcubeu > Add to Bpm list....
  3. Write Rainbow Games and press Ok. At the bottom a new column, Rainbow Games, appears with Rainbow Games - Curcubeu; on the graph a new square in its colour; among the bands Rainbow Games, linked to it.
  4. To add more chases: right-click them in the Library > Add to Bpm list... and write Rainbow Games again: they are added at the end of the list.
  5. Try it: right-click the new square. Next Step lights, the status line says started, the beams take the first step of Curcubeu. Hold the right button: the chase plays on; release: the beams go dark (no Fade yet).
  6. Tick Fade on the column if the colours should flow and stay between the beats.
  7. Put the square where the bass is: drag it over the bars of 45 - 114 Hz, at about half the height; make it as wide as two or three bars with its bottom-right corner.
  8. Choose the input, press Start, play music and watch the square: higher if it lights all the time, lower if it never lights.
  9. Save the project: the list, the square and its place are kept.
