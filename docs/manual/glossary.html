Glossary

For: FullPlayer

The words of this help, in short. The links lead to where they are explained.

WordMeaning
.lfi2, .lfiThe file of a fixture profile; .lfi is the older kind. See Files and settings.
.x1, .xRA project file: .x1 of LumiDMX X1, .xR of LumiDMX Remake Full. See Opening and saving a project.
Address (DMX address)The first channel a fixture listens to; it is set on the fixture and in the patch. See DMX in a few words.
Art-NetDMX sent over a computer network.
BPMBeats per minute: the speed of the music, which Sound2Light follows.
BPM listA list of chases that Sound2Light steps through with the beat.
ChannelOne of the 512 values of a universe; also one function of a fixture (Pan, Dimmer, Gobo ...).
Chase, light sequenceSteps of light played one after the other; what most buttons of the Live panel start.
Cue stepOne step of a light sequence: the values of the channels, with a fade time and a wait time.
Default (of a preset)The DMX value sent when the preset is chosen.
DMX, DMX512The signal that controls the fixtures: 512 channels on one line.
DMX-INDMX that comes into the program, from Art-Net or a Soundlight interface.
FadeA smooth change from one value to the next, instead of a jump.
Fine channelPan Fine, Tilt Fine: the second channel of a 16 bit pan or tilt, for smooth and exact movements.
FixtureA light or an effect machine controlled by DMX: moving head, beam, wash, LED bar, blinder, smoke machine.
FlashA button that works only while it is held down.
FreezeHolding the light as it is, until Freeze is pressed again.
GoboA pattern cut into a disc in the beam of a spot or a beam light, projected with it.
HTP, LTPHow two sources on the same channel are mixed: Highest Takes Precedence (the higher value wins) or Latest Takes Precedence (the last one to change wins).
InterfaceThe device (or the network) through which the DMX leaves the computer.
Live panelThe pages of buttons, sliders and pictures of a project, played in Mixer & Live.
MacroA named list of commands. See Macros and the Command Line.
Out loopThe interfaces playing a short recording of the running buttons in a loop while the program is free to work (in Freeze's place, Option).
PatchThe list of the fixtures of a project with their profiles, universes and addresses. See Setup & Patch.
PresetA range of DMX values of a channel, with a name and a picture (white, gobo 3, strobe ...). See Channels and presets.
ProfileThe description of a fixture: its channels and presets, in a .lfi2 file. See LumiDMX Fixture Editor.
ShowLight sequences placed on a time line. See Show Editor.
Sound2LightThe music drives the lights. See Sound2Light.
TerminatorA plug with a 120 ohm resistor at the end of a DMX line.
UniverseA set of 512 DMX channels.
