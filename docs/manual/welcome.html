LumiDMX Remake Help

Last update: 24 Nov 2026

For: FullPlayer

LumiDMX Remake controls DMX lights: moving heads, beams, washes, LED bars, blinders, smoke and haze machines. It opens the projects of LumiDMX X1 and plays them live: the buttons of the Live panel start chases, colours, gobos and movements, the music can drive them (Sound2Light), and controllers such as the APC mini can play them by hand.

This help explains how a project is made and how it works, so that you can play a project as it is, change its parameters and settings, or make it your own. Each chapter starts with what you see on the screen and then shows how to use it.

How to read this help

  • Full and Player. Under the title of each page, the badges say which program has what the page describes: Full LumiDMX Remake Full, Player LumiDMX Remake Player. See The programs.
  • Numbered pictures. The numbers in a picture (1, 2 ...) are explained in the numbered list under it, in the same order.
  • Names on the screen are written in bold, as they appear: Setup & Patch, Design Live-Panel. A path through menus is written with >: File > Import.
  • Keys look like this: Ctrl+S.
  • Files look like this: dmx_simulator_settings.json.

The chapters

  • Getting started - DMX, the programs, activation, the main window, projects.
  • Setup & Patch - the fixtures of a project, their addresses, the DMX interfaces, MIDI.
  • Mixer & Live - the Live panel, the General Mixer, the tools around them.
  • Show Editor - shows: chases and effects placed on a time line.
  • Sound2Light - the music drives the lights.
  • 2D View - the fixtures on a plan.
  • 3D Previsualisation - the light in 3D before the show.
  • Remote, controllers and MIDI - playing by hand.
  • Macros and the Command Line - commands, alone or in a list.
  • LumiDMX Fixture Editor - the fixture profiles (.lfi2).
  • Troubleshooting, Glossary, What's new.
